## InteractiveChat

ThunderChat's interactive-chat subsystem is an independent Paper-native adaptation of ideas and implementation patterns from **InteractiveChat** by LOOHP.

- Project: https://github.com/LOOHP/InteractiveChat
- Author: LOOHP
- License: GNU General Public License v3.0
- License source: https://github.com/LOOHP/InteractiveChat/blob/master/LICENSE

The InteractiveChat project provides item, inventory, ender-chest, player-name, command, mention, cooldown, and configurable placeholder functionality. ThunderChat adapts these concepts to its own Paper 1.21+ architecture, messages system, permissions, network channels, and MiniMessage pipeline.

Where source code from InteractiveChat is incorporated or modified in ThunderChat, the relevant source files are marked as modified GPLv3-derived work and remain available under GPLv3.
